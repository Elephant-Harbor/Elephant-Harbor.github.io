# About — copy draft (Maren)

## What Elephant Harbor is
Elephant Harbor is a parent company that builds and operates independent businesses. Ventures keep their own brands and customer relationships.

## Why we exist
Useful companies are hard to start and harder to run well. We exist to find problems that matter, build around them, and operate what earns the right to continue.

## How we think
Autonomy for ventures. Hands-on operation when it matters. Long-term orientation. Curiosity about what’s next — without mistaking novelty for progress.

## Accountability
Elephant Harbor is a real operating company with human accountability for commercial and security commitments. We do not publish internal tooling details here.
