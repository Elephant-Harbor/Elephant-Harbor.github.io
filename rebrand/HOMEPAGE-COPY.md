## How we work
We start with a problem, not a predetermined product. We test assumptions quickly, invest when evidence supports it, and change direction when it doesn’t.

Evidence before large commitments. Fast product work. Technology leverage. Disciplined capital. Long-term operation of companies that earn it.

Not every idea deserves to become a company. We stop pursuing opportunities that don’t earn further investment.

## How we operate
Our teams use software and autonomous AI extensively to research, build, analyze, and operate. Human oversight remains where judgment, accountability, security, or commercial commitments require it.
